Regolith Detection:

First terminal Launch RealSense Camera:
source /opt/ros/jazzy/setup.bash
ros2 launch realsense2_camera rs_launch.py

Second terminal Run Detection script:
source /opt/ros/jazzy/setup.bash
python3 regolith_detect.py


Detection Pipeline:
-HSV color thresholding
-filtering
-countour clustering
-regolith pile localization

Purpose:
Used to isolate and estimate the location of regolith pile for future UR3e excavation and sweeping operations
